# Firm tiers

These tiers are an editorial browsing aid for quant internship applicants, not a
ranking of compensation, interview difficulty, investment returns or every team
within a firm. Reviewed October 2026. A less familiar name is not necessarily a
weaker employer.

- **Tier 1: core quant targets.** A deliberately short shortlist: Jane Street,
  Citadel, Citadel Securities, Hudson River Trading, Jump Trading, Optiver,
  IMC, SIG, DRW, Two Sigma, D. E. Shaw, XTX Markets and Five Rings.
- **Tier 2: additional established and specialist targets.** Includes systematic
  funds, smaller research-led firms and broader finance employers with quant
  teams. AQR, Millennium, Point72, Qube, Radix, Squarepoint, Tower, Virtu,
  G-Research, Bridgewater, BlackRock, Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan sit here rather
  than automatically receiving Tier 1 based on size or a finance brand.
- **Tier 3: wider search.** The remaining regional, specialist and adjacent-market
  firms. This is a broader discovery bucket, not a statement that their jobs pay
  less or are easier to obtain.

Five Rings moves into the core shortlist; Radix's two boards now share Tier 2
instead of putting the same employer in two tiers. Small research firms remain
worth considering: [Five Rings](https://fiverings.com/) describes integrated
quant/developer/trader teams, while [Radix](https://radix-trading.com/) describes
a research-led electronic trading business. Those sources establish their focus,
not an objective rank; the shortlist above is a product choice.

Every board belonging to one firm uses the same tier. Tier changes preserve the
user's enabled/disabled board choices. Big Tech retains its separate FAANG+,
Frontier AI and Startups groups.
